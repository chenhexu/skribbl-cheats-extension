Skribbl Auto Guesser helps you find hint-matching words and turn uploaded pictures into drawings on skribbl.io. Search by spelling or meaning, review suggestions and image previews, and choose when to send guesses or start drawing.

HINT-AWARE WORD SEARCH
The panel reads the game's hint, including revealed letters and multiword lengths. Every suggestion must fit that hint. Type part of a word for ordinary text matching, or enable Related words to find spelling, category and meaning-based matches. For example, animal can find eligible animals, related parts or phrases; fish can find related ocean life.

Keep Closest first checked to rank matches, or turn it off for alphabetical order. Green, yellow and red indicate relevance; hover to see why a word matched. Relevance is not a prediction of the correct answer. No hint yet? The panel waits for one. A new drawing round clears your search.

CHOOSE HOW TO GUESS
Click a suggestion, press Enter for the first result, or use 1-9 for the corresponding visible result. Start enables automatic guessing at your chosen delay; Stop stops it. Game feedback confirms delivery, and spam warnings pause sending so rejected attempts are not silently treated as accepted guesses. The game's sending limits still apply.

IMAGE AUTO-DRAWER
Upload or drop an image, choose Auto settings and click Process image. Inspect the original, simplified colors, detected lines, final drawing and animated drawing path before starting. During your own drawing turn, use Start drawing in the Skribbl panel; Stop cancels the run.

Auto adapts color and processing settings to each picture while leaving Outline detail under your control. Manual edits turn Auto off and keep its other suggested values. Use up to 24 simplified colors per layer, adjust color merging, or try Amplify colors to lift dark hues before mapping to the game's palette. The selected color count is a maximum, not a guaranteed number of distinct colors.

For landscapes, choose Full image / scenery without subject selection. For an object, enable Use main subject and choose Subject only or Subject first + simplified background. Process image finds a subject automatically when needed. Mark subject and Edit subject parts let you correct missing pieces manually. Cropping is a separate choice.

Fast/Balanced speed and the drawing target control the tradeoff between time and detail. Plans may omit small features to fit the target; preview statistics show the result. Drawing uses trusted browser input and displays Chrome's debugger notice while active.

Suitable thick lines use a matching wider pencil to reduce thin contour tracing and bucket clicks. The planner checks the brush footprint and keeps bucket filling when an irregular shape cannot be reproduced safely.

AI ON YOUR COMPUTER
Open Local AI setup and choose Inside Chrome - GPU acceleration, Inside Chrome - CPU, or the optional Python terminal service. Chrome modes include the models in the extension: no terminal, paid AI account or API key is required. Load the models before playing, check each feature's readiness and test word-search speed on your device. First loading can take several seconds. GPU support depends on your browser and graphics driver; CPU is available as an alternative.

Local AI supplies semantic word suggestions, foreground masks and optional line detection. Browser line detection, curated categories, Auto settings and manual selection work without neural inference. AI may miss parts or return weak associations; the preview and selection tools let you correct it.

WORKSPACE AND PREFERENCES
Drag the drawer out of its dock to expand it, resize from any edge or corner, or open the large image viewer for cropping and previews. Both panels can sit flush against the browser content edges. Drawing starts from the Skribbl panel. Local AI setup chooses the engine shared by both. Drawing speed, target, subject/background mode, line detector and color amplification are remembered. Download a preview PNG or plan JSON when needed.

PRIVACY AND ACCESS
Content scripts run only on skribbl.io. Chrome AI uses packaged models on your device. No hosted inference provider, analytics collector or API token is used. Guesses and drawing input go to Skribbl, and optional public word-list downloads contact GitHub/jsDelivr. Python mode sends images or search inputs only to your own service at 127.0.0.1:8765 after you grant access. Preferences and custom word choices are stored locally.

Storage remembers preferences and the AI engine; Debugger performs drawing you start on the game canvas. Optional Offscreen access is requested when enabling Chrome AI and hosts its worker. The optional Python companion is installed separately from the source project. See the linked privacy policy for full details.

GETTING STARTED
1. Open or refresh skribbl.io after installation.
2. For terminal-free AI, open Local AI setup, select Chrome CPU or GPU and click Load AI models.
3. Search the current hint's word bank, or upload an image in Auto-Drawer, process it and inspect the preview.
4. Send a selected guess or start drawing during your own turn.
